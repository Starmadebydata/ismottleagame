Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mottle a game or just a word?

Both. Mottle – Hide & Seek is a browser camouflage game created by Star Lord on 8x.gg, and mottle is a valid 6-letter English word meaning spotted or blotched coloring.

Can I play Mottle on mobile without downloading an APK?

Yes. No APK or download is needed. Both the Hide & Seek arena and the Word Challenge above work natively in mobile Safari and Chrome with touch controls.

Why does this site have a custom 3-biome version instead of an 8x.gg iframe?

The 8x.gg server sets Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors, which blocks third-party websites from embedding it in an iframe. We built a native HTML5 Canvas edition with 3 biomes, Ghost Stealth bonuses, and a Word Challenge so you can play immediately without a broken frame.

Is Mottle Game the same as Motel Manager Simulator?

No. Search engines sometimes confuse “Mottle” with “Motel” simulator games. Mottle – Hide & Seek is a camouflage and firefly-collection game.
