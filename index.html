How to Play Mottle – Hide & Seek

01

Make Your Move

Drag with your mouse or one finger (or hold WASD / Arrow keys) to glide toward a glowing firefly. Moving makes you vivid and easy for Seekers to spot.

02

Become the Background

Release your input to freeze in place. Your Camo meter rises rapidly, shifting your color to match the mottled ground beneath you.

03

Outlast the Seekers

Avoid getting caught in sweeping Seeker light cones while moving. If your Alert meter hits 100%, the round ends. Clear waves of fireflies to advance!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mottle a game or just a word?

It is both. Mottle – Hide & Seek is a browser stealth/camouflage game created by Star Lord on 8x.gg, and mottle is also a real 6-letter English word meaning spotted or blotched coloring.

Can I play Mottle on mobile without downloading an APK?

Yes. You do not need to download any standalone APK or installer. The HTML5 game above works directly in mobile Safari and Chrome with one-finger touch drag, and the original 8x.gg listing also runs in the browser.

Why does the page have its own playable version instead of an 8x.gg iframe?

The original 8x.gg game server sends a Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors header that blocks third-party websites from embedding it in an iframe. Building a native HTML5 Canvas edition lets you play immediately without a blank frame or login wall, while still linking to the original on 8x.gg.

Is Mottle Game the same as Motel Manager Simulator?

No. Search results sometimes mix up “Mottle” with “Motel” simulator games due to spelling similarity. Mottle – Hide & Seek is a camouflage and firefly-collection game, not a motel management sim.
